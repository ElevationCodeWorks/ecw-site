A website is never finished. Software it depends on goes out of date, security fixes pile up, and a small problem can quietly cost you customers for months.

I look after sites and customer-facing apps on a regular schedule, and I take over projects that have stalled. I start with an honest review of what's there, then fix what matters first. When something old needs replacing, I modernize it in steps so the business keeps running.
