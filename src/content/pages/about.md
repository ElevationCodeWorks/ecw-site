## My background

I've spent 14 years building and modernizing software. Most recently I've been a lead software engineer at a large enterprise, building platforms used by millions of customers and leading AI adoption across engineering teams.

Earlier, I spent nearly five years as a contract engineer on federal projects, including work for the FDA, the Department of Veterans Affairs and the U.S. Census Bureau. That work taught me to build software that holds up under real scrutiny: secure, tested and maintainable long after launch.

## How I use AI

I've trained hundreds of enterprise engineers on AI-assisted and spec-driven development, and I work the same way for every client. Before I build anything, I write down what the software must do and you approve it. AI speeds up the work, and I stay accountable for every decision and every result.
