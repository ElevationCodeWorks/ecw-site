## The project

What Can I Do? helps people find concrete ways to act on the issues they care about. Visitors search by location and get actions they can take right away: places to donate or volunteer, things to learn, and products to buy from proven solutions.

## What I did

Important, Not Important contracted me to build the first version of What Can I Do? and launch it to production. The product has grown since that engagement. This case study covers the initial build and launch.
