## The project

Condies answers one question quickly: when are conditions best? It shows the current weather, the next twelve hours, and a week of temperature ranges, then picks the best day and time to head out and scores it out of 100.

## Details

- Saved spots and a map, so regular destinations are one tap away.
- Installs on a phone's home screen and opens like a native app.
- Light and dark themes that follow the device setting.
